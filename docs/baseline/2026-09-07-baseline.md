## 9. Architecture Findings Recorded for Future Phases

The following findings are intentionally recorded without modification in this baseline:

1. Entity taxonomy/data coverage is broader than current dedicated renderer coverage.
2. Relationship rendering is not fully context-aware.
3. Relationship labels are partly hard-coded in frontend code.
4. Source and citation concepts are not fully separated.
5. Citation numbering is not modeled as document-local Research metadata.
6. Evidence location/provenance is not yet audit-grade.
7. Research schema v1 and v2 coexist.
8. Existing Research content and promoted/canonical claim mappings are not yet fully reconciled.
9. Atlas and Research do not yet share a single canonical entity URL resolver.
10. Atlas rendering is heavily concentrated in `assets/js/atlas.js`.
11. Research/Atlas content is currently substantially client-rendered.
12. Generated-index policy is not yet uniform across all registry/index files.
13. `jsonschema.RefResolver` is deprecated.
14. Local development runtime versions differ from the versions explicitly used by parts of CI configuration.
15. Existing automated tests do not yet cover all identified relationship, provenance, identity, citation, and renderer-context requirements.
